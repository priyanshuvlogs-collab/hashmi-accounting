# Real-review policy

This site only publishes reviews that can be attributed to a public source.

## Rules

1. No lorem ipsum and no stock “CEO / VP Marketing” quotes from the previous WordPress theme.
2. No invented names, job titles, companies, star counts, or dollar results.
3. Quote the review as written. If a reviewer wrote “Syed,” the site says “Syed.”
4. The federal corporation lists **Fahad Hashmi** as director. Confirm a preferred personal display name before using one as a headline or byline. Until then, use the firm name.
5. Do not invent CPA or other designations next to a review.
6. Negative public reviews exist. The marketing site may highlight attributable praise; it must not claim a perfect record.
7. Link the source. Current quotes are taken from [ThreeBestRated’s Pickering tax-services listing](https://threebestrated.ca/tax-services-in-pickering-on).

## Display name note

Public reviews mention Syed. Corporate filings mention Fahad Hashmi. Those are not treated as interchangeable marketing copy. Confirm before a personal name is used in the header or about hero.

## Where this is implemented

- `src/lib/reviews.ts` — the only review objects rendered on the site
- `/privacy#reviews` — visitor-facing version of this policy
