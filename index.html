Questions owners ask first

      

        

          How much does it cost?
          
It depends on transaction volume, payroll size and which returns you need. After the first call we send a fixed monthly quote in writing, and that is what you pay.

        

        

          My books are behind. Can you still help?
          
Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping is common. We quote it separately, bring everything up to date, then move you onto the monthly routine.

        

        

          Do I need to change accounting software?
          
Usually not. We work in Xero, QuickBooks and Sage. If you are on spreadsheets, we will suggest the simplest option for your size.

        

        

          Can you deal with the tax authority for me?
          
Yes. Once you authorise us as your agent we handle correspondence, submit returns and respond to queries on your behalf.

        

        

          How do I switch from my current accountant?
          
Tell us who they are. We send a professional clearance request and collect your prior-year files, so you do not have to chase anything.
